✦ FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I start a project?+

Choose your services in the estimator or contact CreativeByte directly. Share your requirements and we will discuss the scope and quote.

Can I request changes?+

Yes. Revision rounds can be included depending on the package and project scope. The final revision policy will be confirmed before work begins.

Do you work with businesses and students?+

Yes. CreativeByte can work on websites, posters, videos, photos and digital projects for different types of clients.

How is the final price decided?+

The estimator provides a baseline. Complex features, number of pages, content requirements and turnaround time can affect the final quote.

Can you make a custom package?+

Absolutely. If your project needs multiple services, contact us and we can build a package around the actual deliverables.
